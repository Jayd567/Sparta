Features

What you'll find here

A quick rundown of what the server's about.

Survival

Mostly vanilla, with a few carefully picked additions. No pay-to-win — everything's earned in-game, not bought.

Land Claims

Claim your base so other players can't grief it. Outside your claim, the world stays open for anyone to explore.

Rules

Keep it simple

A short list, that's it.

  • 1
    Be respectful. — treat other players the way you'd want to be treated.
  • 2
    No griefing or stealing. — unless it's part of an agreed-on raid or PvP event.
  • 3
    No cheats or hacked clients. — play with what everyone else has.
  • 4
    Keep chat friendly. — banter's fine, harassment isn't.

Join the community

Server news, event announcements, trading, and a place to chat before you even log in. Most of the community hangs out here.
